Job Title: Operations Manager / Team Lead (Administration & Training)
Job Description:
We are looking for a motivated Operations Manager / Team Lead with strong experience in team leadership, administration, training, and performance management. The ideal candidate will have experience managing large teams, improving workforce performance, and coordinating day-to-day operations.
Responsibilities:
Lead and supervise large teams across administrative and operational functions
Improve team performance through coaching, training, and development programs
Plan and coordinate workflows, schedules, and operational processes
Design and deliver training programs in leadership, communication, and team building
Manage departmental budgets and resource planning
Conduct investigations, resolve conflicts, and provide employee counseling
Build strong working relationships with team members and stakeholders
Prepare reports and presentations for management
Requirements:
Proven experience in team leadership and management
Strong communication, counseling, and conflict resolution skills
Experience in training & development and curriculum design
Ability to manage budgets and administrative operations
Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and office tools
Strong organizational and problem-solving skills
Nice to Have:
Experience in structured organizations (e.g., military, large enterprises)
Background in workforce planning and performance improvement
Public speaking or presentation experience
